SAMPLE STARTUP IDEA PROMPTS FOR TESTING
========================================

1. An app that uses AI to match college students with internships based on their GitHub activity and class projects, bypassing traditional resume screening.

2. A subscription service that delivers pre-portioned ingredients for culturally authentic meals from a different country each week, with recipe videos from native home cooks.

3. A B2B SaaS tool that automatically detects and redacts PII from internal Slack messages and documents before they're shared with third-party vendors.

4. A peer-to-peer parking marketplace where homeowners rent out their driveways by the hour in dense urban neighborhoods, like Airbnb for parking spots.

5. A mobile app that gamifies physical therapy exercises for post-surgery recovery patients, with real-time form feedback using the phone camera.

6. A platform that connects freelance translators with small e-commerce businesses to localize product listings for international markets in under 24 hours.

7. An AI-powered legal document reviewer for small businesses that flags risky clauses in vendor contracts without needing a lawyer.

8. A social app for plant parents to trade cuttings and seeds locally, with care guides and a community Q&A for rare houseplants.

9. A SaaS dashboard that aggregates all sustainability metrics (carbon, water, waste) for manufacturing SMBs and auto-generates ESG compliance reports.

10. An on-demand tutoring platform where high school students can book 15-minute micro-sessions with verified college students for quick homework help.
